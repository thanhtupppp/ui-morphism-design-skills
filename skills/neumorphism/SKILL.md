# Neumorphism

## Intent
Make controls appear raised from or pressed into a shared surface through soft light and dark shadows.

## Rules
- Use a stable base surface and one consistent light direction.
- Pair a light highlight shadow with a dark contact shadow.
- Reserve strong color for semantic states.
- Use inset shadows for pressed states and explicit outlines for focus.
- Best for compact knobs, toggles, media controls, and thermostat widgets.
- Add labels, icons, values, and border/focus treatment to every control.

## Avoid
- Full-page monochrome relief.
- Hidden focus rings.
- Dense forms, tables, and navigation made entirely from soft shadows.
